# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation describes how OctoAcme delivers cross-functional projects through customer-first, iterative, data-informed practices with clear ownership and continuous learning.

## Project management processes summary

OctoAcme begins with initiation: teams validate the business need, define measurable goals and success criteria, identify stakeholders, outline resources and risks, and decide whether the work should move into planning. Planning then turns an approved initiative into an actionable delivery plan by creating and prioritizing a backlog, adding acceptance criteria, estimating scope, defining the Definition of Done, identifying dependencies, and agreeing on milestones and a release plan.

During execution, teams track work on a project board and use standups, delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone demos to maintain alignment. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, dependencies, meetings, documentation, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers build and test solutions; and QA contributors and stakeholders help validate quality and acceptance. Teams maintain a risk register, share regular status updates through a common source of truth, and escalate blockers through the defined path when needed.

Quality is built into delivery through small pull requests, code review, acceptance criteria, automated tests, linting, security scanning, CI, and manual or end-to-end testing where appropriate. Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria and checks, prepare release notes and rollback plans, validate in staging, deploy, verify production behavior, and communicate the release. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, retrospectives capture lessons and turn them into owned, time-bound improvement actions that are followed up over time.
